# Work Order 029 (WO-029): Draft Studio, Deadline Engine (Adli Tatil & HMK Rules) & CI/CD K8s Alignment

## Objective
Implement Draft Studio backend & frontend with versioning and export capabilities, enhance the Deadline Engine with Turkish legal calendar (Adli Tatil / HMK Art. 104 and statutory holidays) and lawyer review stages, expand the CI/CD pipeline, and align Kubernetes deployment manifests with actual service ports and health probe endpoints.

## Tasks & Deliverables
1. **Draft Studio & Export Implementation (Task 11)**
   - Create backend CRUD & versioning in `apps/api/routers/draft_studio.py` with optimistic locking (`expected_version`).
   - Implement `EXPORT_DRAFT` worker job triggering PDF/DOCX export.
   - Implement frontend `DraftStudioShell.tsx` and connect it to live backend endpoints in matter details tab.

2. **Deadline Engine Enhancement (Task 12)**
   - Enhance `apps/api/services/deadline_engine.py` with Turkish Judicial Recess (Adli Tatil: 20 Temmuz - 31 Ağustos) rules under HMK Art. 104 (automatic extension to September 7).
   - Implement Turkish national statutory holidays and religious holiday calendars with automatic business day roll-over.
   - Implement lawyer review workflow stages (`submit_for_review`, `approve_deadline`, `reject_deadline`) and jurisdiction checks (`TR_HMK`, `TR_IIK`).

3. **CI/CD & Kubernetes Alignment (Task 13)**
   - Replace dummy assertions in `apps/api/test_api.py` with real `/health/live`, `/health/ready`, and `/api/v1/system/dependencies` integration probe tests.
   - Align Kubernetes deployment manifests (`k8s/api-deployment.yaml`, `k8s/web-deployment.yaml`, `kubernetes/pilot-deployment.yaml`) with real service ports (API 8001, Web 3000) and health probe paths.
   - Update `.github/workflows/ci.yml` to execute parallel jobs for backend tests and frontend linting/building.
